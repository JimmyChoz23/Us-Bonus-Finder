Getting started

How it works

US Bonus Finder helps visitors discover third-party promotions. The provider, not this website, controls the entry process and any reward.

1. Browse the listings

Choose a category and review the offer description, supported device, geographic restrictions and any special conditions.

2. Read the provider's terms

When you choose to continue, you will leave US Bonus Finder and visit a third-party offer page. Read its privacy notice, official rules, fees, trial terms and eligibility requirements before submitting information.

3. Decide whether to participate

Only submit information if you understand who is collecting it, how it may be used and whether the offer involves marketing communications, subscriptions or other obligations.

4. Follow up with the provider

Questions about an entry, reward, account, cancellation or personal information should be directed to the provider named on the offer page.

No guaranteed outcome: A listing or click does not mean you have won a prize, qualified for a reward or been approved for a product.
