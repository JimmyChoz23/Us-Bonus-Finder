Eligibility & device requirements

Offer access is determined by each third-party provider. The listings supplied to us specify United States targeting and device restrictions.

Geographic eligibility

The current listings in this directory are marked for the United States. Visitors outside the United States should not attempt to access or complete these offers unless the provider's official terms expressly allow it.

Device requirements

  • Android: Nielsen Pulse, Jersey Mike's, Sneakers, Gas and Experian listings.
  • Desktop: Both Cash App-themed promotions, Nike and ShopBack listings.
  • iOS: Walmart-themed gift-card entry promotion.

Device detection on this website is an estimate based on the browser user agent. It is not a verification system and does not override the provider's requirements.

Age and other requirements

Some promotion pages state that entrants must be US residents aged 18 or older. Other requirements may apply. Check the individual offer's official rules and disclosures.

Do not use false information

Do not use VPNs, fabricated addresses, another person's details or other methods to bypass eligibility, geographic or device restrictions.
