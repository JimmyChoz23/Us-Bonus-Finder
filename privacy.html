Information collected by this website

This starter website does not contain a registration form and is not designed to collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, payment details or government identifiers.

Local click measurement

When a visitor clicks an offer link, the browser may store a limited click record in local storage on that device. This record contains the offer identifier, a simple traffic-source label from the page URL (if supplied), and a timestamp. It is stored locally and is not sent to US Bonus Finder by this static template.
